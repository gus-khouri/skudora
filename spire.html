Deep Spire Integration. Real Capabilities.

Quote → Sales Order → Purchase Order

Quotes created in Skudora push into Spire as sales orders with a single action. Purchase orders follow — fully synced, with live status flowing back so your team and customers always see the authoritative state. No manual reconciliation, ever.

Self-Serve Customer Onboarding

Customers create their own accounts, browse your catalogue, and request quotes without a single minute of paid admin time. If a prospect never converts, nothing is lost. When they do, their data flows directly into Spire — no re-entry, no duplication. Rate limiting and automated abuse prevention keep the front door open without the risk of spam or account flooding, so your team stays focused exclusively on work that generates revenue.

Accounting, Tax & Compliance

Every transaction that originates in Skudora lands in Spire fully formed — tax rules applied, GL mappings intact, audit trail preserved. Compliance obligations and document attachments carry through the full transaction lifecycle without manual intervention.

Multi-Location Inventory

Skudora surfaces Spire's multi-location inventory data across your entire digital operation — displaying accurate per-location stock levels and aggregate quantities for every SKU. Whether inventory spans one warehouse or many, orders are intelligently sourced from the right location in real time.

Intelligent Vendor Fulfillment

When a sales order arrives for a product that makes sense to fulfill directly from a vendor, Skudora automatically generates the corresponding purchase order — ready for review, not creation from scratch. Your team approves rather than builds, vendors ship direct to the customer, delivery is faster, and your business isn't burdened with carrying inventory it doesn't need to hold.

Purchase Order Receiving

Receive purchase orders line by line through Skudora — capturing serial numbers, lot numbers, and expiry dates at the point of receipt. Each received item reconciles instantly against the open PO in Spire, inventory updates in real time, and all traceability data is stored against the transaction permanently.
