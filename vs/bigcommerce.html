The real difference

One system, not two systems talking

Other platforms keep their own copy of your products, customers and orders, and middleware syncs that copy with Spire. When a sync lags or fails, your website and your books disagree. Skudora works directly on Spire's data through Spire's own API, and extends it where Spire stops. There's no second copy to keep in sync.

Build on what you already have

Your catalogue, customers, pricing and warehouses already live in Spire. Skudora starts there. Every feature is included from day one, and you turn modules on as your team is ready. No big launch, no retraining everyone at once.

B2B shouldn't be an upgrade

BigCommerce's quoting, invoicing and company accounts live in B2B Edition, which has no public price and is sold with its Performance plan, starting at US$1,499 a month billed annually. In Skudora, quotes, invoices, statements and everything else are included at $549 CAD a month.

Price 100,000 SKUs without a spreadsheet

Skudora's pricing engine calculates a list price for every SKU from what it actually costs you to sell it. When a supplier's price or the exchange rate changes, your prices follow in real time.

  • Purchase currency and exchange rateCosts from foreign suppliers convert at today's rate.
  • Country of originPricing accounts for where each item comes from.
  • Category and subcategory marginsSet the margin once for a whole category.
  • Margin exceptionsOverride the margin for specific brands or items.
  • Multi-vendor costsCompare what each vendor charges for the same SKU.
  • Handling surchargesDangerous goods and large, awkward items carry their extra shipping and handling cost.

MAP rules that override the math

Set minimum advertised price rules by brand or manufacturer. When the calculation comes in below MAP, Skudora holds the advertised price at MAP. BigCommerce relies on add-on apps for MAP.

Show these prices to the public, or only to certain types of customers once they log in.

Questions distributors ask

Can BigCommerce connect to Spire?

Yes, through a third-party connector or custom middleware. That setup keeps a second copy of your data in BigCommerce and syncs it with Spire. Skudora is built directly on Spire's API, so there's nothing to sync.

Doesn't BigCommerce B2B Edition already do quotes and invoices?

Yes. B2B Edition offers quotes with expiry dates, an invoice portal and net terms. It's sold with the Performance plan and still needs middleware to talk to Spire. Skudora includes quoting, invoicing and statements, all working on your Spire data, for $549 CAD a month.

Do I have to migrate my products and customers?

No. Skudora builds on the catalogue, customers, pricing and warehouses already in Spire.

Will my team be forced to use AI?

No. AI shows up inside specific tasks when someone chooses to use it. Nothing runs on its own.

Where is my data stored?

Skudora's own data is stored in Canada. Payment processors, and any third-party add-ons or header snippets you choose to install, store data under their own terms and may keep it outside Canada.
